Proposed delivery structure

A Nine-person delivery model for digital mapping and related services.

Our team brings together nine professionals with expertise spanning software development, GIS and digital mapping, UX and service design, data analysis, research and content, quality assurance, and business support. This multidisciplinary structure enables us to approach projects from both technical and business perspectives, combining practical delivery capability with user-focused design, data-driven insight, quality assurance and operational support. We can assemble the right combination of skills for each engagement, whether supporting a business, public-sector organisation or other client with software development, digital services, data and analytics, mapping, process improvement or technology-enabled projects. Our approach is collaborative and flexible, with team members working across disciplines to deliver reliable, maintainable and practical solutions aligned with each client's objectives.
