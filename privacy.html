Privacy

Information handling for this website.

This page provides a plain-English description of the intended approach for a small corporate website. It is not a substitute for a project-specific privacy notice, controller/processor agreement or other legal documentation where those are required.

Contact information

If you contact the company, information you choose to provide may be used to respond to your enquiry, manage a potential business relationship and maintain appropriate business records.

Data minimisation

Only information reasonably needed for the stated business purpose should be collected. Unnecessary personal information should not be submitted through the website.

Third-party services

The static site is designed without mandatory analytics or advertising scripts. Hosting, domain, form and email providers may process technical or contact information under their own terms.

Retention and security

Business records should be retained only as long as appropriate for the purpose and legal obligations. Access should be limited to authorised personnel and sensitive information should not be exposed in public source code.

For public-sector projectsWhere a buyer requires personal-data processing, the applicable roles, purposes, categories of data, retention periods, security measures and contractual terms should be documented before processing begins.
