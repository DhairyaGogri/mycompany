Tender and Procurement

Digital capability built for professional procurement

DHAIRYA & SMIT GOGRI LTD supports organisations through the planning, design, development and delivery of technology-enabled projects across software, data, digital services, mapping, research and business support. We work with clients across the public, private and third sectors and can adapt our delivery approach to suit different project sizes, procurement models and organisational requirements. Our multidisciplinary team combines technical delivery with user experience, data analysis, research, quality assurance and business support, allowing us to address projects from both a technical and operational perspective.

Our delivery principles

Clear understanding of requirements

We begin by establishing what the client is trying to achieve, who the users are, what constraints exist and what a successful outcome looks like.


Proportionate technology

We select technologies and approaches according to the needs of the project rather than applying unnecessary complexity.


Security-conscious delivery

Security is considered throughout development and implementation, including appropriate access controls, secure handling of information, sensible architecture and responsible data practices.


Accessibility and inclusion

Where digital services are user-facing, accessibility and inclusive design are treated as core delivery considerations rather than an afterthought.


Quality and reliability

We use structured testing, review and validation to improve the reliability and maintainability of delivered solutions.


Transparent communication

Clients receive clear communication around progress, requirements, risks, decisions, dependencies and deliverables.


Value-focused delivery

We seek to deliver practical outcomes that provide measurable or demonstrable value rather than technology for its own sake.


Maintainability

Solutions are designed with future updates, operational requirements, documentation and handover in mind.
