Designed around the published Witney digital interactive map brief.

West Oxfordshire District Council published the current UK4 notice on 3 September 2026. The published notice describes a digital mapping service focused on curated exploration, storytelling, navigation and Witney's local character.

Evidence boundaryThe public notice does not publish a detailed supplier qualification schedule, mandatory insurance thresholds, named personnel requirements or a full technical specification. The site therefore does not claim those unpublished items as proven facts; those must be checked in the procurement portal's tender documents.

Supplier qualifications

The published notice does not state specific mandatory qualifications, accreditations, insurance limits or financial standing tests. Those items should be extracted from the tender pack in In-Tend and added only once verified.

Technical / functional detail

The published notice gives the outcome-focused brief but does not expose a full functional specification. The site therefore presents a credible delivery architecture and demonstrator without claiming that the council has already approved those exact implementation details.
